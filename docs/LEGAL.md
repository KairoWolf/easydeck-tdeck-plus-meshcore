# Safety and legal notice

Please read this before relying on EasyDeck for anything important.

## EasyDeck is not safety or emergency equipment

- The **SOS** feature is not a certified distress beacon. It is **not** a PLB, EPIRB or satellite
  messenger, and it does **not** contact 000 or any other emergency service.
- An SOS only reaches people on the LoRa mesh whose devices are on, in range, and listening.
  Delivery depends on terrain, weather, repeaters, batteries and other people's equipment, and
  **is never guaranteed**. Messages, location updates and "I'm OK" check-ins can be late or lost.
- **In an emergency call 000** (or 112 from a mobile). If you go somewhere without mobile
  coverage, carry a registered **PLB** or a satellite messenger as well. Don't rely on EasyDeck as
  your only way to get help.
- Battery estimates, GPS positions, distances and the map can be wrong. Don't navigate or make
  safety decisions with EasyDeck alone.

## Weather, warnings and other data

Weather forecasts and warnings come from a community bot (it uses Open-Meteo, the Bureau of
Meteorology and NSW agencies). They are for information only, may be out of date or incomplete,
and are **not official warnings**. For fire, flood, storm or other emergencies, follow the
[Bureau of Meteorology](https://www.bom.gov.au), [NSW RFS / Hazards Near Me](https://www.rfs.nsw.gov.au),
[NSW SES](https://www.ses.nsw.gov.au) and local authorities.

## Radio regulations

You are responsible for using your radio legally where you are. In Australia, LoRa devices in
915–928 MHz operate under the ACMA *Radiocommunications (Low Interference Potential Devices)
Class Licence*: stay within its power and band limits, and use suitable antennas. Other countries
have different bands and limits. Pick the right radio preset for your region and don't increase
transmit power or change frequencies beyond what your local rules allow.

## Privacy

- Location sharing, "I'm here" messages, SOS and adverts with a position **broadcast where you
  are** over the radio. Anyone who can decode them, and maps that collect adverts, may see it.
- Public and hashtag channels can be read by anyone who knows the channel name. Private channels
  are only as private as their key.
- EasyDeck stores your messages, contacts and settings on the device and SD card. Anyone with the
  device can read them unless you set a screen lock.
